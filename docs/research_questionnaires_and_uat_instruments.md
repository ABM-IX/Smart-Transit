# SmartTransit – Research Instruments & User Evaluation Questionnaires 🇧🇼
**Project:** Smart Transit – Real-Time Multi-Modal Public Transit Coordination Platform  
**Target Pilot Area:** Gaborone & Greater Gaborone Transport Corridors (Botswana)  
**Document Purpose:** Academic Research Ethics, Pre-Implementation Baseline Survey, Driver & Commuter Viability Questionnaires, and User Acceptance Testing (UAT) Evaluation Instrument.

---

## 🏛️ Part 1: Research Ethics & Informed Consent Statement

> **Note for Field Researchers:** Read or provide this statement to every participant (commuter or driver) prior to questionnaire administration.

### Participant Information & Consent Form
**Study Title:** Digital Two-Sided Coordination and AI-Assisted Routing in Urban Public Transportation (Gaborone Case Study).  
**Investigator:** Smart Transit Project Research Team.  

**Purpose of the Study:**  
The purpose of this study is to investigate the existing challenges in Gaborone's public transportation system (combis, special taxis, and buses) and evaluate the feasibility, usability, and operational impact of the **Smart Transit** mobile and dispatch platform.

**Confidentiality & Voluntary Participation:**  
- Your participation is entirely voluntary. You may decline to answer any question or withdraw at any time without penalty.
- All information collected will be kept strictly anonymous and confidential. No personal identity (e.g., national identity number, real name) will be published.
- Data collected will only be used for academic analysis, research publication, and software improvement.

**Consent Declaration:**  
> *"By proceeding with this survey, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this consent form and agree to participate in this research study."*

## 🎙️ Part 4: Key Informant Qualitative Interview Guide

*(For in-depth semi-structured interviews with transport association representatives, rank marshals, and pilot participants)*

### Questions for Transport Marshals & Association Leaders:
1. What are the biggest operational bottlenecks at the Gaborone Central Bus Rank and feeder ranks today?
2. How do drivers currently manage departure intervals, and what leads to combi bunching along arterial roads (e.g., Kudumatse, Nelson Mandela Drive)?
3. What regulatory or operational concerns do operators have regarding digital dispatch and location tracking?
4. How can a system like Smart Transit assist in reducing passenger complaints and improving overall fleet efficiency?

### Questions for Pilot Commuters:
1. Describe your typical morning and evening commute experience in Gaborone.
2. In what situations would you consider taking a multi-modal trip (e.g., combining a special taxi hail with a scheduled combi or long-distance bus)?
3. How did your experience using the Smart Transit app prototype compare with conventional commuting without real-time data?
