## 🎯 Academic Staging Strategy ("From Scratch" Progression)

> **Academic Integrity & Delivery Policy for the Team:**  
> Although prototype code and services have been engineered for feasibility verification, the formal project deliverables **must be documented and submitted strictly as an incremental, phase-by-phase engineering lifecycle**. Presenting an already-completed system at Project Review 1 will raise examiner concerns regarding academic authenticity.  
> 
> Therefore, team submissions must follow the formal four-stage academic progression:
> 1. **Project Review 1 (1–7 Sept 2026):** Focus exclusively on **Problem Definition, Requirements Specification, Literature Review, and System Methodology**. The system is presented as a *proposed architecture*.
> 2. **Project Review 2 (18 Sept – 5 Oct 2026):** Focus on **System Design, Architectural Modeling, Visual Paradigm UML (Class/Sequence), Relational ERD, and Dashboard Wireframing**.
> 3. **Project Review 3 (5–19 Oct 2026):** Focus on **System Implementation, Telematics Integration, Test Cases, Performance Validation, and User Acceptance Testing (UAT)**.
> 4. **Final Submission & Defense (23 Oct – 17 Nov 2026):** Deliver the **Final Comprehensive Technical Report, Research Poster, and Live Demonstration**.
