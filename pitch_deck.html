The Reality on the Ground

The Urban Transit Crisis in Gaborone

Millions of productive citizen hours lost every year to an informal information blackout

⏳ The 45-Min Blackout

Commuters wait at BBS Mall, UB, or Station with zero visibility. You never know if the next combi is 2 minutes away or 40 minutes away.

Result: Chronic tardiness for work and classes.
🚐 Combi Bunching

Without coordination, 3 combis cluster into a platoon racing for the same passengers. The lead combi fills up, the next two run empty, followed by a 40-minute service void.

Result: Massive fuel waste and driver frustration.
🧩 Fragmented Journeys

Taking a neighborhood special cab in Block 8 to catch a combi to the Bus Rank for an intercity coach to Francistown requires 3 completely blind negotiations.

Result: Opaque taxi fares and missed bus departures.
⚠️ Paratransit accounts for over 85% of motorized trips with zero digital scheduling

System Architecture & Product

SmartTransit: The 3-Tier Intelligent ITS

A coordinated, full-stack paratransit platform connecting all stakeholders

📱 1. Commuter Mobile App

Cross-platform Flutter application for passengers:

  • Live combi map tracking & stable ETA countdowns.
  • Spatial on-demand hailing for special cabs.
  • Multi-modal trip planning (combis + cabs + buses).
  • Automatic boarding via co-movement geofencing.
🧭 2. Driver In-Cab Telematics

Zero-distraction HUD tailored for paratransit drivers:

  • 1 Hz background GPS coordinate streaming.
  • Dynamic Spacing Advisory (SLOW DOWN / MAINTAIN).
  • Instant nearby passenger hail notifications.
  • Fair metered fare calculation preventing disputes.
🖥️ 3. Regulatory Command Center

Desktop web console (React + Leaflet) for DRTS:

  • Live bird's-eye view of all vehicles and corridors.
  • Corridor velocity, bunching alerts, & demand heatmaps.
  • One-click statutory DRTS compliance reports (CSV/JSON).
  • Zero-install access for government workstations.
⚡ Powered by Python FastAPI, WebSockets at 1 Hz, and Supabase PostGIS

Algorithmic Innovation

Active Anti-Bunching Headway Control

We don't just passively show where combis are—we actively regulate how they space out

❌ Without SmartTransit (Passive Apps like Vaya)
[Combi A] ═ 50m ═ [Combi B] ════════════════════ 12km ════════════════════ [Combi C]

Passive tracking apps only display clustered icons on a map. Drivers continue racing each other, burning fuel, and leaving commuters waiting 40 minutes for the next empty combi.

✅ With SmartTransit (Newell-Daganzo Regularization)
[Combi A] ════ 4km ════ [Combi B] ════ 4km ════ [Combi C] ════ 4km ════ [Combi D]

Our engine computes real-time inter-vehicle headway \(h_i\) and streams visual/audio badges into the driver's cab: "SLOW DOWN / HOLD 60s" or "MAINTAIN GAP".

✨ Speed-Floored ETA ($v_{\text{floor}} = 18\text{ km/h}$): ETA countdowns never jump to infinity when combis dwell at traffic lights or roadside stops!
🔬 Mathematically grounded in operational paratransit research

NetworkX Graph Routing with Backward Scheduling

If a traveler must catch a 07:00 AM bus to Francistown, our algorithm works backwards: subtracts 15-min luggage buffer, selects the optimal combi feeder run, and schedules the neighborhood cab pickup at 06:12 AM automatically.

Transparent Aggregated Fares

Commuters see the total trip cost upfront: (e.g., P35 Special Cab + P8 Combi + P120 Coach = BWP 163.00), eliminating unfair driver overcharging and roadside price disputes.

Why a Desktop Web App?

A 6-inch smartphone cannot monitor 50 combis across 4 municipal corridors. Desktop browsers provide multi-variable filtering, dual-monitor map inspection, and instant spreadsheet integration with zero software installation.

Statutory Audit Automation

Replaces error-prone manual paper clipboards with one-click official exports for route licensing audits, speeding violations, and corridor capacity planning.
